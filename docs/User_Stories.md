# User Stories

## ReFood Connect – Smart Food Waste Redistribution Platform

---

## Authentication

### US-01
**As a** user, **I want** to register an account **so that** I can access the platform.

**Priority:** Must Have

**Acceptance Criteria**
- User can register successfully.
- Required fields are validated.

---

### US-02
**As a** registered user, **I want** to log in securely **so that** I can access my dashboard.

**Priority:** Must Have

**Acceptance Criteria**
- Valid credentials allow login.
- Invalid credentials display an error.

---

### US-03
**As a** user, **I want** to reset my password **so that** I can regain access to my account.

**Priority:** Should Have

---

## Food Donor

### US-04
**As a** food donor, **I want** to create a food donation **so that** surplus food can be redistributed.

**Priority:** Must Have

---

### US-05
**As a** food donor, **I want** to upload food images **so that** NGOs can verify the donation.

**Priority:** Should Have

---

### US-06
**As a** food donor, **I want** to specify food quantity and expiry time **so that** NGOs know whether the food is usable.

**Priority:** Must Have

---

### US-07
**As a** food donor, **I want** to edit or cancel my donation **so that** incorrect information can be updated.

**Priority:** Should Have

---

### US-08
**As a** food donor, **I want** to track my donation status **so that** I know whether it has been collected.

**Priority:** Must Have

---

## NGO

### US-09
**As an** NGO, **I want** to browse available food donations **so that** I can request food.

**Priority:** Must Have

---

### US-10
**As an** NGO, **I want** to request a donation **so that** food can be reserved for beneficiaries.

**Priority:** Must Have

---

### US-11
**As an** NGO, **I want** to track the delivery status **so that** I know when food will arrive.

**Priority:** Must Have

---

### US-12
**As an** NGO, **I want** to manage my received donations **so that** I can organize food distribution.

**Priority:** Should Have

---

**Priority:** Must Have

---

### US-14

**Priority:** Must Have

---

**Priority:** Must Have

## Beneficiary

**Priority:** Must Have

---

### US-18
