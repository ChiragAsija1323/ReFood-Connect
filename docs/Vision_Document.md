# 1. Project Overview
## Introduction
ReFood Connect is a web-based platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting food donors with organizations and individuals who need food. Every day, restaurants, hotels, supermarkets, hostels, and event organizers dispose of surplus edible food, while many NGOs and underprivileged communities struggle to access sufficient meals.
The platform acts as a centralized system where food donors can list available surplus food, NGOs can request donations, volunteers can manage pickups and deliveries, and administrators can monitor the complete donation process. By improving coordination among stakeholders, ReFood Connect aims to reduce food wastage, increase transparency, and ensure that surplus food reaches beneficiaries efficiently.
---
# 2. Problem Statement
Food waste is a major global issue. Large quantities of safe and edible food are discarded every day due to excess preparation, unsold inventory, or cancelled events. At the same time, thousands of people face food insecurity and depend on charitable organizations for daily meals.
Most food donation activities are managed manually through phone calls or messaging applications, making the process slow, unorganized, and difficult to track. This often results in delayed pickups, spoiled food, and missed donation opportunities.
There is a need for a centralized digital platform that simplifies food donation, automates coordination between donors and NGOs, tracks deliveries, and improves transparency throughout the redistribution process.
---
# 3. Vision Statement
To build a reliable, secure, and user-friendly platform that minimizes food waste by efficiently connecting food donors, NGOs, volunteers, and beneficiaries, ensuring that surplus food reaches people in need quickly and safely.
